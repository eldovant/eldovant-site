      An independent house for pictures and sound.

      ELDOVANT develops and produces original films, short films, series and music. Each work is written, directed, cut and scored as one piece.

      Motion pictures

      Films, short films and series built on a premise, a turn and an ending. Trailers and first looks only for productions that exist.

      Music

      Scores, themes and original compositions — made for a picture, or strong enough to stand without one.

      Each production chooses its own tools, artificial intelligence among them. Direction, continuity and final approval stay with the author.

      About the studio

      Every frame answers to the one before it. Every cut has a reason. Every sound arrives on purpose.

      ELDOVANT makes films and music by one rule: nothing stays in the picture, or in the mix, unless the story needs it. Genres will change. The standard will not.
